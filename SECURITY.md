# Security

Omart gossips plugin listings to pals and accepts meet, part, publish, and retract from a logged-in browser session.

## Report a vulnerability

Use a private [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/ahlmark/omart-desk/security/advisories/new).

Please do not open a public issue for:

- bypassing the `x-omart` header or the origin check
- reading another ship's pal list without a login
- forging a listing as a ship that did not publish it
- crashing the agent with a gossip fact or a poke

Include the desk version, what you sent, and what the ship did. You do not need a full exploit.

## What happens next

The maintainer will confirm receipt, look at the report, and follow up on the advisory. A fix ships in this repository and on the publisher ship before any public write-up.
